First appearance of Spiderman

Spider-Man first appeared in Amazing Fantasy #15, published by Marvel Comics in August 1962.

Key Details

  • Release Date: August 1962 (cover-dated August 1962)
  • Creators: Written by Stan Lee and drawn/inked by Steve Ditko
  • Main Character: Peter Parker, a high school student who gains spider-like powers after a radioactive spider bite.
  • Major Plot Point: The issue introduces his origin story, the tragic murder of Uncle Ben, and the lesson that "with great power there must also come great responsibility".

The direct sequel to Spider-Man's debut is The Amazing Spider-Man #1, which launched in March 1963.
